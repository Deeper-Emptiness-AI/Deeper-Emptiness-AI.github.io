Care

Features

Voice first — chat and touch, too.

Just talk and it's done. Prefer not to speak? There's a text chat, and big, simple buttons for everything else — nothing is locked behind a keyboard.

🎙️

Voice-first companion

Talk naturally about your day, ask health questions, or just chat. Healthya remembers your context and follows up like a good companion — warm, patient, never rushed.

🍽️

Meal tracking

Snap a photo — or just say what you ate. AI reads the food, estimates nutrition, and suggests a balanced next meal.

💊

Medication reminders

Scan a pill bottle or prescription for drug info, then get reminders and daily adherence tracking.

🌅

Morning briefing

A daily start with weather, real news headlines, a health tip, and today's appointments — plus weekly and monthly recaps of your health.

🩺

When you're unwell

Describe how you feel and get plain-language guidance on what to do next — then find nearby clinics and pharmacies. Not a diagnosis.

🧩

Daily brain games

Four light puzzles — sudoku, crossword, bubble pop, and color sort — for a gentle daily mind workout.

🌄

3-Day Wins

Set a small goal and keep it for three days at a time, with a gentle reminder and a home-screen widget to cheer you on.

💧

Hydration & exercise

Gentle water reminders and light activity tracking that fit into an ordinary day.

⌚

Connected health apps

Optionally read sleep, steps, heart rate, blood pressure, and other health metrics from Apple Health or Google Health Connect — read-only, and only if you turn it on.

🚑

Emergency help

Offline first-aid guides and a big 911 button — one tap from the home-screen widget. The app opens your phone's dialer and you place the call; calling 911 from the app alerts your linked family, with your location if you've turned that on. Healthya is not an emergency response service.

The family app

Close, without hovering.

A separate companion app lets a linked family member support an older adult — only ever seeing what the senior has consented to share.

  • Summaries, not transcripts — daily and monthly overviews with at most a short quoted phrase, never full conversations.
  • A generalized activity area on about a 2 km grid, plus one latest location kept for emergencies — no route history, ever.
  • An alert to every linked family member when the senior calls 911 from the app.
  • Every share is decided by the senior — set when approving the link, and can be turned off anytime.

Trust

Privacy & safety

Health data, treated like it matters.

Privacy is built in from the first screen — with clear consent, strong safeguards, and no advertising business hiding underneath.

No ads, no data sales

We never sell or share your personal information for advertising, and never work with data brokers.

You control every share

Health sharing, activity area, and emergency location are separate per-family switches — the senior sets them when approving a link, and can turn any of them off at any time.

Health-app data stays read-only

Data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect is read-only, never sold, and never used for ads.

US data residency

Data is stored in US-region infrastructure with encryption and per-user row-level isolation.

Built around US privacy law

Designed for CCPA / CPRA, Washington's My Health My Data Act, Illinois BIPA, and related state laws.

Download your data

Exercise your right to know — request a copy of your data from the senior app's Privacy Policy screen, one export every 30 days.

Delete anytime

Delete your account in the app for immediate removal, or request it on the web — gone within 30 days at the latest.

Healthya supports daily well-being and is not a medical service or an emergency response service. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical care. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
