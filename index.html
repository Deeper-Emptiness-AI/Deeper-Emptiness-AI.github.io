03 — 3-Day Wins

Thirty days is a wall.
Three days is a hill.

Every goal in Healthya is a chain of three-day hills — three, nine, twenty-one or thirty days of them. Miss one day and the hill holds. Miss two in a row and only the hill you're standing on resets. What you've already climbed never goes backwards.

The hill

One small thing, three days

Pick the days of the week it applies to and up to two reminder times — or none at all. A home-screen widget draws the ridge you're on, so it's there before you unlock the phone.

Mercy

Slipping is built in

There is no failed state anywhere in the model. One missed day changes nothing, and your total check-ins and the hills you've crossed are never taken away.

The nudge

Answer without opening anything

Done, or not yet — straight from the notification. “Not yet” quietly moves it an hour later, once, and never into your sleep.

06 — optional

Close, without hovering.

A separate Family app lets you manage care for someone you're linked with — a parent, a grandparent, or any adult in your family — seeing only what they have consented to share.

  • Summaries, not transcripts — daily and monthly overviews with at most a short quoted phrase, never full conversations.
  • A generalized activity area on about a 2 km grid, plus one latest location kept for emergencies — no route history, ever.
  • An alert to every linked family member when they call 911 from the app.
  • Every share is decided by the person being cared for — set when approving the link, and can be turned off anytime.

07 — privacy & safety

Health data, treated like it matters.

Privacy is built in from the first screen — with clear consent, strong safeguards, and no advertising business hiding underneath.

No ads, no data sales

We never sell or share your personal information for advertising, and never work with data brokers.

You control every share

Health sharing, activity area and emergency location are separate per-family switches — set when approving a link, and can be turned off at any time.

Health-app data stays read-only

Data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect is read-only, never sold, and never used for ads.

US data residency

Data is stored in US-region infrastructure with encryption and per-user row-level isolation.

Built around US privacy law

Designed for CCPA / CPRA, Washington's My Health My Data Act, Illinois BIPA, and related state laws.

Download your data

Exercise your right to know — request a copy of your data from the Healthya app's Privacy Policy screen, one export every 30 days.

Delete anytime

Delete your account in the app for immediate removal, or request it on the web — gone within 30 days at the latest.

Healthya supports daily well-being and is not a medical service or an emergency response service. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical care. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
